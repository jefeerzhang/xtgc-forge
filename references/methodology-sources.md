# 方法论参考来源

本 skill 在设计 Step 2c(gap 裁定)/ Step 3(主题涌现)/ Step 4(假设提炼)时参考了下列**公开学术来源**。仅作为方法论参考,**不复述任何受版权保护的条款**。

## 文献综述方法论

- **PRISMA 2020** — 系统性综述报告规范
  - 协议:CC BY 4.0(可商用、可改编)
  - 参见:https://www.prisma-statement.org/
- **JARS(Journal Article Reporting Standards)** — APA 期刊文章报告标准
  - 协议:APA 出版,部分受版权保护
  - 参见:https://apastyle.apa.org/jars
  - 注意:本 skill 不复制任何具体条款
- **DA-RT(Data Accessibility Research Transparency)** — 数据可访问性与研究透明度
  - 参见:https://www.dataaccessibilityresearch.org/

## 因果推断方法论

- **Pearl DAG(Directed Acyclic Graphs)** — 因果图模型
  - Pearl, J. (2009). *Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference*. Cambridge University Press.
  - 参见:https://bayes.cs.ucla.edu/BOOK-2K/
- **VanderWeele 反事实框架** — Explanation in Causal Inference
  - VanderWeele, T. J. (2015). *Explanation in Causal Inference*. Oxford University Press.
  - 参见:https://www.oup-usa.com/
- **Hernán & Robins Causal Inference: What If** — 因果推断教材
  - 协议:开源可访问
  - 参见:https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/causal-inference-book/

## 效应量与功效分析

- **SESOI(Smallest Effect Size of Interest)** — 最小实质效应量
  - 参见:Lakens et al. (2018) "Improving Inferences about Null Effects"
- **Cohen's d / 效应量家族** — 经典统计教材
  - Cohen, J. (1988). *Statistical Power Analysis for the Behavioral Sciences*

## 社科方法论(综合)

- **Creswell, J. W.** *Research Design: Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed Methods Approaches*(2014)
- **Booth, A.** *Clear and present questions: formulating questions for evidence based practice*(2006)
- **Matt Pocock skills**(MIT)— `grill-me` / `wayfinder` / `handoff`
  - 参见:https://github.com/mattpocock/skills

## 假设打磨方法论(三层假设闸)

- **Carlini 结论优先测试** — "How to Win a Best Paper Award"
  - 参见:https://nicholas.carlini.com/writing/2026/how-to-win-a-best-paper-award.html
  - 借鉴:RS2(结论优先)/ RS3(单句金句)/ RS4(最险假设 1 周可测),经经管实证语境改写
- **researcher-pack**(MIT)— research-strategy principles
  - 参见:https://github.com/andrehuang/researcher-pack
  - 借鉴:结论优先测试 / 单句金句 / 最险假设 + 快速验证路径,只借鉴理念不复述原文
